# Fixer-Upper Watch — Nova Scotia + New Brunswick

Goal: catch **every new listing** that could be your next renovation project,
the moment it hits the market, free and automatically.

The reliable way to do this is **saved searches with email/app alerts** on each
site. Set each one up once; new matches get pushed to you forever.

---

## 1. REALTOR.ca (the MLS — most complete)
- Go to **realtor.ca**
- Search area: set to **Nova Scotia**, then repeat for **New Brunswick**
- Apply filters (see below), then click **"Save Search"** / turn on **email alerts**
- Do it twice: one saved search for NS, one for NB

## 2. Kijiji (best for FSBO + private fixer deals)
- **kijiji.ca** → Real Estate → Houses for Sale
- Search "fixer upper", "handyman special", "needs work", "as is"
- Locations: Nova Scotia, then New Brunswick
- Tap **"Save search"** → enable notifications (Kijiji emails/app-pushes new hits)

## 3. Facebook Marketplace (where private/off-market deals show up)
- Marketplace → Property For Sale
- Set location + radius to cover Halifax/Dartmouth and your NB target areas
- Search the keywords below; check **daily** (FB alerts are weak)
- Join local groups: "Nova Scotia Real Estate", "New Brunswick Homes for Sale", FSBO groups

## 4. Brokerage sites (also free alerts)
- **Royal LePage, RE/MAX, Century 21, Viewpoint.ca** — each lets you save a
  search and email alerts. Viewpoint.ca is NS-specific and very detailed.

---

## Filters to use (fixer-upper hunt)
- **Property type:** House / Detached (skip condos unless you want them)
- **Price:** set a ceiling = your expected cash from the India St sale
  (e.g. your ~50% share + financing room)
- **Keywords:** `fixer upper` · `handyman special` · `needs work` · `TLC` ·
  `as is` · `renovation` · `estate sale` · `tear down` · `investment`
- **Sort by:** Newest first
- **Days on market:** new listings (so you pounce before others)

## What makes a good flip/reno target (quick gut-check)
- Cosmetically rough but **structurally/sound bones** (foundation, roof age)
- Good location > perfect house — you can fix the house, not the street
- Priced low because of *cosmetics or a motivated seller*, not because of
  something unfixable (flood zone, major structural, contaminated, no clear title)
- Room to add value: extra bedroom potential, basement, lot/density upside

## Timing reminder (from the sale plan)
Window-shop now, but **don't put a deposit on a fixer until your India St sale
is firm** (signed, conditions cleared). Paid-off seller = you have the luxury of
waiting for the right buyer AND the right fixer.
